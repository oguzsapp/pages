Shed support

Save links in one tap. Clear them weekly.

Contact

Write to oguzs.app@gmail.com. There is no account to look up, so say which phone and which version of Shed you use.

Suggestions and problems

In Shed, open Settings and choose "Send feedback", or tap the link at the end of Review. Write your message and press Send. It asks for no name or email and we do not answer individual messages; to talk to us, write to the address above. Nothing from your archive is added.

Sorting with AI

Shed asks the first time you open it whether links may be sorted with AI. To change your answer, open Settings and turn "Sort links with AI" on or off. With it off, nothing is sent to be sorted.

Moving to a new phone

Your links live only on your phone and are left out of cloud backups. In Settings, choose "Export your archive", keep the file, and on the new phone choose "Import from a file".

Deleting your data

Removing a link deletes it. Uninstalling Shed deletes everything it stored; nothing is kept on a server. See the privacy policy.
